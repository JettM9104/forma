New to This?

Don't know what you're doing?
We got you.

Domains, hosting, DNS — it sounds like a foreign language. Here's the plain-English breakdown of what you actually need to get your site live.

🌐

Your Domain

Your domain is your web address — the yourname.com part. You register it through a registrar like Namecheap or Google Domains and pay a small yearly fee (usually $10–$20/yr) to keep it.

☁️

Hosting

Hosting is the server that stores your website files and serves them to visitors. Without it, your site has nowhere to live. Popular options include Netlify, Vercel, and Cloudflare Pages — many have generous free tiers.

🔗

DNS

DNS is the "phone book" of the internet — it connects your domain name to your hosting server. We handle the configuration for you, but the records live with your domain registrar or host.

💡

Just so you know: We build your website — we don't provide hosting or manage your domain for you. Once your site is delivered, you'll need a hosting service and a domain registrar, both of which charge their own recurring fees. Don't worry though — we'll walk you through exactly what to sign up for and get everything connected.
